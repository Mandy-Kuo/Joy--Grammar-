Today’s mission

Save the Earth! 🌱

We will talk about plans and things we must do.

What do you plan to do?

2 min

Plan = something you want to do

Subject + plan(s) to + verb
I / You / We / They
They plan to plant more trees.
He / She / It
Tammy plans to use her shopping bag.
2 min

Have to = something necessary

Subject + have / has to + verb
I / You / We / They
We have to sort garbage.
He / She / It
Ken has to recycle batteries.

Past: everyone uses had to.
