Today’s mission

What is it like?

We will use our five senses to say what something seems like.

Look, listen, smell, taste, and feel!

Concept

Five senses, five verbs

👀
look
👂
sound
👃
smell
👅
taste
✋
feel

Use the sense in the question again in the answer.

Build the pattern

Ask and answer

What does + one thing + sense verb + like?

It + sense verbs + like + noun.

What do + plural things + sense verb + like?

They + sense verb + like + noun.

Teacher model

Look and feel

👀 look
What does the rock look like?

It looks like a head.
✋ feel
What does the fruit feel like?

It feels like a rock.
Remember
one thing → does / it / verb + s
Teacher model

Sound, smell, and taste

👂 sound

👅 taste
